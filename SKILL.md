## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a private notebook sketch of one favored character or object. Keep it important without showing off, cute without sweetness, ornate without clutter, and confident without a posed stance. Use loose imperfect marks, bold broken ink contours, thin watercolor, and a large amount of untouched white paper. Preserve silhouette, character, rhythm and expression rather than detail. The subject stays small and may sit off-center, at the edge, or cropped. Stop before the drawing is complete. Take two to four soft colors from the source and use them sparingly. It is not a filled illustration, a smooth digital finish, a plastic render, a fluorescent candy palette, or a stacked commercial title.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-244/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
